ADMISSIONS

LCWU offers undergraduate and graduate admissions.

For Fall 2026 undergraduate admissions, admission is based provisionally on First Year marks. Admission is confirmed after submission of the complete Second Year result and maintaining the required eligibility criteria.

Applications for undergraduate admissions are submitted online.

The university publishes admission notices, merit lists, admission regulations, deadlines, fee information, test patterns, quota information and other admission updates on its official website.

HOSTEL

LCWU provides hostel facilities for students coming from other cities and abroad.

The university has hostel facilities for Intermediate, Graduate, Post-Graduate and PhD students.

Because accommodation is limited, hostel seats are allocated through merit.

Students must apply for hostel accommodation through the prescribed admission process.

Hostel admission is generally offered for one academic year. Extension depends on university rules, conduct and academic performance.
